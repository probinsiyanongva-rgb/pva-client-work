About this course

Understanding the work

A client sends a request. Before you start, you need to know exactly what's being asked, what "done" looks like, and whether anything is missing. When you're finished, you need to know it's actually right before you say so.

By the end of this course, you should be able to say: "I understand what needs to be done, I know what I need to check, and I know what to do when something is unclear."

In practice, that means you can:

  • read a client request and write down the task, the finished result, the sources, the deadline in your time, the limits and the open questions
  • tell which gaps you can fill yourself and which only the client can answer
  • decide whether to go ahead, ask first, or pass a decision to the client
  • plan the work in a sensible order and follow a client's checklist or process
  • check your work against the request before you say it's done, and handle revisions without breaking what was right

This course builds on Communication & Professionalism, which covers how to write to clients. Here you work out what needs to happen; when a message is needed, the lessons point you back to that course. The tasks use simple lists, files and sheets, so no specialist VA skills are needed.

Names, email addresses and companies in the examples are made up. The example screens are simplified drawings.
