      What You Did on Your Own

      In this lesson you found Pine Candle's price yourself, worked out the deadline, left out Juniper Lane and the discontinued plate, and got all four sheets moving while waiting only on the answers you actually needed. You asked Grace two things only she could answer, and passed her the one request that wasn't yours to grant. That's what working on your own means here: figure out what you reasonably can, keep the work moving, and ask when the instruction really doesn't let you go ahead safely.

      When something does need to go to Grace, the wording is in Communication & Professionalism: the clarifying-question pattern for your questions, the escalation guidance for Paper Moon's request, and the completion guidance for telling her the sheets are ready.

      Key Takeaway
      • Read the whole request and write your Task Card before you open the files.
      • Sort what's missing before you ask, and ask early.
      • Make the call for the whole job and for each part.
      • Check against the Task Card before you say it's done.
